Mvolt Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Mvolt ("the app") is a set of everyday calculators, file tools and study courses. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

What stays on your device

The app has no accounts and no sign-up. Your settings, saved tools, Skill Hub progress, notes, XP and certificates are stored only on your device. The app does not send them to us. Apart from the AI service described below, we do not run servers that collect your data. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes them.

Files you convert

Photos, PDFs, documents and spreadsheets you choose in the file converter are processed on your device. They are not uploaded to us.

AI features

When you use an AI feature, the text you type (or the photo you choose to scan) is sent over the internet to our AI service, which passes it to Anthropic to produce an answer. We do not store your text, photos or the answers. To manage free daily AI credits, our service keeps counters linked to a random device ID and your network address. Daily counters are deleted automatically after about two days. Bonus credits you earn are kept for up to 60 days. Do not enter sensitive personal information into AI features. AI answers can be wrong, so check important facts. If you add your own Anthropic API key in settings, your requests go directly from your device to Anthropic instead, and the key stays on your device. Anthropic's privacy policy applies to the data it receives.

Third-party resources

To show fonts and to load file conversion libraries, the app may fetch files from Google Fonts, jsDelivr or cdnjs. Those services can see your IP address and device details as part of a normal web request.

Permissions

The app uses the internet. Your device may ask for access to files or photos only when you choose a file to convert.

Ads

The free version of the app shows ads provided by Google AdMob: a banner at the bottom of the screen and optional rewarded video ads that you can watch to earn extra AI credits. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and other device and usage data to show and measure ads, and may show a consent form where the law requires it. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Google's privacy policy applies to the data it collects.

Mvolt Pro subscription

You can buy an optional Mvolt Pro subscription, which removes ads and raises the daily AI limit. Payments are handled by Google Play; we never see your card details. We use RevenueCat to confirm whether a subscription is active. It receives a random app user ID and your purchase status.

Usage counts

To understand which tools are used, the app sends our server the name of a tool you open together with your random device ID. It does not include anything you type, any files or any photos. Daily counts are kept for about ten days.

Children

The app is for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.
